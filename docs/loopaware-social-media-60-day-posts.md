# LoopAware 60-Day Social Media Post Calendar

This document provides two social posts per day for 60 days. Each post body is written to stay under 300 characters before adding links, handles, images, or hashtags. If a platform link or hashtag is required, replace copy instead of appending to it.

## Campaign Guardrails

- Use one post body per social post.
- Pair posts with product screenshots, dashboard crops, short clips, or architecture visuals when available.
- Keep claims limited to current LoopAware surfaces: feedback widget, subscriber capture, traffic analytics, uptime checks, LA Sentry, role-aware access, reports, mobile feedback, and self-hosting.
- Rotate the problem angle instead of repeating the same feature pitch.
