Frequently asked questions

What counts as a "revenue-generating project"?
Any product, service, or website that generates income, directly or indirectly. This includes SaaS products, e-commerce sites, ad-supported content, and internal tools at for-profit companies. Personal blogs, open-source projects, and student work are free.
Can I try Pro features before paying?
Yes. Sign up for the free tier and use it as long as you like. When your project starts generating revenue and you need higher limits, upgrade to Pro.
Why is the Free tier so generous?
LoopAware is in early access. We want developers to use the full product and give us feedback. The Free tier covers all features for non-revenue projects. When your project starts earning, a Pro license keeps you covered and supports continued development.
Do you offer refunds?
Yes. If you are not satisfied within the first 30 days, contact us for a full refund.
How does self-hosted licensing work?
The source code is available on GitHub under a source-available license. Non-revenue projects can use it freely. Revenue-generating projects need a commercial license, which includes a license key for your deployment.
