Why top pages come first

A small team cannot improve every page at once. Page-level traffic shows which URLs deserve attention before the team spends time rewriting copy, investigating feedback, or adding deeper instrumentation.

LoopAware stores bot traffic but excludes it from default analytics views so common reports stay focused on human visits.

Questions it answers

What did visitors see?

Identify the pages that actually received traffic during a selected interval.

Where should feedback be read first?

Pair message source context with traffic volume to prioritize review.

Which launch pages worked?

Compare page reach after publishing a campaign, waitlist, or documentation update.

Limitations

  • Top pages reporting is page-level traffic context, not session replay.
  • It does not replace product analytics for deep in-app funnels.
  • For exported row-level analysis, use traffic CSV export.

Best fit

Use top pages reporting to decide where a small site team should spend review and improvement time first.
