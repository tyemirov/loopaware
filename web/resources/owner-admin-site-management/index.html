What managers own

Site managers own the boundary between public collection and private review. That includes allowed origins, widget settings, team membership, health monitors, traffic schedules, and protected LA Sentry tokens.

Read-only team members can review site signals but do not get these management controls.

Common management jobs

Create and update sites

Register public domains, names, and allowed origins for the sites LoopAware should monitor.

Assign access

Add team members for read-only review and keep manager-only actions restricted.

Operate settings

Configure widget display, subscriber forms, traffic schedules, uptime checks, and LA Sentry setup.

Limitations

  • LoopAware site management is not a full CRM or support-seat system.
  • Global admin access should remain an operator-controlled deployment decision.
  • Separate unrelated products or clients should use separate sites.

Best fit

Use this when one operator account needs to manage several public signal surfaces with clear role boundaries.
