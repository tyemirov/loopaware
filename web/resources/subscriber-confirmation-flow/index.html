Subscriber confirmation flow

LoopAware's public subscribe endpoint creates subscriber records that can move through pending, confirmed, unsubscribed, or deleted states with token-backed confirmation and unsubscribe pages.

Why confirmation belongs in the flow

Subscriber capture is most useful when the dashboard reflects real intent. Confirmation links help distinguish a submitted address from a subscriber who has completed the sign-up step.

Token pages are public utility pages and remain noindex, while this resource page is the crawlable explanation.

Operating workflow

Collect the address

Embed the subscribe form on a public page with a site ID and origin-bound public endpoint.

Send the confirmation

LoopAware stores the subscriber and routes confirmation through the token-backed confirm page.

Respect unsubscribe

Unsubscribe links let recipients opt out without requiring dashboard access.

Limitations

  • LoopAware captures subscriber interest; it is not a full newsletter delivery platform.
  • Confirmation and unsubscribe pages should not be indexed because they are token utilities.
  • Teams still need to decide what they send subscribers and when.

Best fit

Use this for waitlists, launch updates, and early product interest where the team needs clean subscriber state in the dashboard.
