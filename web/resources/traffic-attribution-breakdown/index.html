What attribution helps decide

When a launch, newsletter, social post, or partner link sends visitors to a site, attribution context helps teams understand which channel produced traffic and which pages those visitors reached.

Attribution is based on request and URL context. It is not an ad network identity graph.

Useful views

Source

See broad origin categories such as direct, referral, or campaign-driven traffic.

Medium

Separate channels when links include campaign context that the traffic pixel can capture.

Campaign

Compare named campaigns against subscriber and feedback activity in the same site window.

Limitations

  • Attribution quality depends on referrer and campaign data being present.
  • LoopAware does not claim cross-device visitor identity or ad-platform reconciliation.
  • Use traffic reports or CSV export when the team needs a broader recurring summary.

Best fit

Use this for practical campaign review on public product, documentation, and landing pages.
