Read-only team access for site signals

LoopAware lets site managers add Google-authenticated team members who can view a site's feedback, subscribers, traffic, health, and LA Sentry data without managing site settings.

Why read-only roles matter

Teams often need shared visibility without shared administrative power. LoopAware keeps team members attached directly to a site so they can review signals while managers retain configuration authority.

Team access depends on the user's Google-authenticated email matching the site team membership.

What team members can review

Customer signals

Read feedback messages, subscriber lists, and mobile feedback context for assigned sites.

Operational signals

View traffic, health status, favicon notifications, and LA Sentry issues.

No management controls

Team members do not manage site settings, team membership, tokens, or schedules.

Limitations

  • This is direct per-site membership, not a reusable organization or group system.
  • Team members should be invited by the Google login address they will use.
  • Managers remain responsible for site configuration and data handling.

Best fit

Use this to let collaborators inspect site signals without granting broad operator control.

Implementation checklist

  • Confirm which operator is the owner and which users need team-member access for the site.
  • Review allowed origins, snippets, traffic reports, uptime checks, and LA Sentry settings before changing ownership or access.
  • Use the dashboard role boundaries to keep management actions with owners and admins.

Common questions

When should teams use this team member site access guide?

Grant read-only LoopAware site access to Google-authenticated team members while keeping site management controls limited to managers. It is useful when LoopAware access needs to match how people actually review and act on site signals.

What should teams verify after setup?

Confirm the workflow is tied to the correct LoopAware site, then check the first dashboard records with the people who will act on them.
