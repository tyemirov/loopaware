Data minimization matters

LoopAware is designed for site operators who need enough visibility to act. The traffic pixel records page visits and bounded browser context, while the dashboard keeps bot traffic out of default totals and makes CSV export explicit.

For teams that need a smaller setup surface, the lightweight analytics page focuses on the pixel install path.

Implementation checklist

  • Install the traffic snippet or no-JavaScript fallback on one public page before expanding to the full site.
  • Confirm top pages, attribution, device, location, or export views show the context the team needs for decisions.
  • Use the dashboard summaries or scheduled emails to decide which pages deserve copy, feedback, or uptime follow-up.

Common questions

When should teams use this privacy first analytics guide?

LoopAware privacy-first analytics track visits, unique visitors, top pages, devices, locations, and attribution signals without forcing small teams into a heavy analytics suite. It works best when the team needs practical page-level traffic context inside the same LoopAware site workspace.

What should teams verify after setup?

Confirm the workflow is tied to the correct LoopAware site, then check the first dashboard records with the people who will act on them.
